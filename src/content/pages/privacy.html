Tailored Air LLC ("we," "us," or "our") operates tailoredair.com. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights with respect to that information. We take your privacy seriously and do not sell your personal data to anyone.

We retain your contact information only for as long as necessary to provide services to you or as required by applicable law. If you would like your information removed from our records, please contact us and we will honor that request promptly.
