Our Values

Tailored Air operates by four core values we call our SUIT: Solve It, Understand, Integrity, and Trust. These guide every interaction our Littleton, CO team has with customers, from the first phone call to the final walk-through. They are the standard we hold ourselves to on every job.

S Solve It

Take initiative and own the outcome. We don't wait to be asked and we don't look for shortcuts. When something needs fixing, we solve it, handle it with care, and hold ourselves to a standard we'd be proud of in any home, including our own.

U Understand

Listen first. Understand what people need, then find the right solution. Every home is different and every customer has a different situation. We take the time to hear you out before we recommend anything, because the right answer starts with the right question.

I Integrity

Do the right thing. Always. No upselling, no hidden fees, no telling someone they need a replacement when a repair will do. Integrity means being the same company whether the customer is watching or not. We say what we mean and do what we say, every single time.

T Trust

Build confidence through reliability and honesty. Trust is earned one job at a time. We earn it by showing up when we say we will, communicating clearly throughout, and leaving every home in better shape than we found it. That is the only standard we hold ourselves to.
