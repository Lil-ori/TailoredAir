HVAC Services in Littleton & the Denver Metro

Tailored Air installs, repairs, and maintains heating, cooling, indoor air quality, and water heater systems for homes and businesses across Littleton, Englewood, Highlands Ranch, Lakewood, and the greater Denver metro area. Every recommendation is explained in plain language. If a repair will do, we will say so.

Heating

Colorado winters are hard on furnaces, boilers, and heat pumps. We install new heating systems, repair the one you already have, and run seasonal tune-ups so you are not finding out it failed on the first cold night. American Standard equipment is our go-to for replacements. We also service every major brand already in the home.

If your furnace is short-cycling, blowing cold air, or making new noises, call before it becomes an emergency. Most repairs can be diagnosed on the first visit.

Cooling

Central air, ductless mini-splits, and smart thermostat upgrades keep Denver-metro homes comfortable when summer heat shows up fast. We size systems for the house you have, not a one-size catalog quote, and we walk you through efficiency options before any work starts.

Spring tune-ups catch low refrigerant, dirty coils, and failing capacitors before the first heat wave. Same-day repair is often available when a unit goes down mid-season.

Indoor Air Quality

Dry winters, wildfire smoke, and older ductwork all show up in the air you breathe. We install and service air purifiers, humidifiers, UV lamps, and carbon monoxide detectors so the system that heats and cools your home also keeps the indoor air cleaner.

If anyone in the house has allergies or you notice dust building up faster than it should, an air-quality visit is usually the fastest way to find the cause.

Water Heaters

We install and repair traditional tank and tankless water heaters. If you are running out of hot water, hearing popping in the tank, or seeing rust at the base, we will tell you whether a repair or a replacement is the honest next step.

Tankless upgrades are a good fit for many Littleton homes that want more capacity without a larger tank. We handle the gas, venting, and electrical work that goes with it.

Commercial HVAC

Offices, retail, and light commercial buildings need systems that stay online during business hours. We design, install, upgrade, and maintain commercial HVAC so tenants and customers are not waiting on a callback.

Maintenance agreements are available if you want seasonal inspections on a schedule instead of waiting for a failure.
